How We Engage

Diagnose first. Then design, advise and measure.

STEP 01

Diagnose

A focused, paid diagnostic: where the company stands, what is constrained and what would move results in the next 90 days.

STEP 02

Design

The plan, the model or the roadmap, with priorities, owners and KPIs defined before execution starts.

STEP 03

Advise

Scope-controlled advisory: monthly reviews, leadership sessions and roadmap adjustments. Execution stays with your team or our Digital Engine.

STEP 04

Measure

The KPIs agreed at the start are reviewed at every stage, so progress is visible rather than assumed.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is the difference between the four offers?

Business Planning & Strategy defines the model and the plan. Fundraising Readiness prepares the investment case. GTM & Growth Roadmap fixes how revenue is won. Market Entry sequences expansion into a new market. Many clients start with one and add another later.

Are engagements fixed-fee?

Yes. Every engagement is fixed-fee and project-based, with scope, deliverables and timeline confirmed in a one-page engagement outline before work begins.

Do you also handle execution?

Our advisory work defines the strategy. Execution, such as website, content, CRM or campaigns, is delivered through our Digital Engine when clients need it, or by the client's own team.

Which companies does Nexus Ways work with?

B2B companies, SaaS and AI startups, scaleups and SMEs in technology, PropTech, FinTech and logistics, with a focus on the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Europe and the Middle East.
