The Problem This Solves

Page two is where demand goes unseen.

Companies on page one capture most search clicks; those on page two receive a fraction. The cause is usually structural: a weak technical foundation, no keyword strategy and content that is not mapped to how buyers actually search.

When Results Appear

SEO compounds. It does not happen overnight.

Search takes months, not weeks. This is what a typical engagement looks like; targets for your site are set against your own baseline.

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Technical fixes

    Crawl errors resolved and the infrastructure ready to receive ranking signals.

  2. Month 1

    First signals

    First articles indexed and Search Console impressions tracked against the baseline.

  3. Months 2–3

    Ranking movement

    Target keywords begin moving into the first pages, and movement becomes trackable.

  4. Months 4–6

    Organic growth

    Organic traffic and first organic enquiries measured against month one.

  5. Month 6+

    Compounding

    Authority builds on itself and search becomes a stable acquisition channel.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Technical fixes take effect within weeks, but ranking movement usually becomes visible in months two to three, and meaningful traffic growth in months four to six. SEO is a compounding channel, not a quick one.

Why start with an audit?

Because monthly investment without a diagnosis is guesswork. The audit shows where you stand, what competitors are doing and what to prioritise, and it is useful on its own even if you do not continue.

Do you work across languages and markets?

Yes. Keyword research and content are built for each target market and language, including English, Turkish and Arabic, rather than translated.

Who owns the content and accounts?

You do. Articles, reports and all Search Console and analytics properties remain yours during and after the engagement.
