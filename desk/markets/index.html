Research framework

What we examine

Five areas of evidence, recorded with source, period, unit and geography — the context an investor needs before looking at a single unit.

  1. 01

    Market activity

    Transactions and sales volumes, with their reporting period and geographic scope.

  2. 02

    Pricing and housing conditions

    Official price indices and comparable indicators, with their methodology and limitations stated.

  3. 03

    Macro and currency context

    Inflation, financing conditions and FX evidence, each with the date and basis of the rate.

  4. 04

    District and location evidence

    Local differences, transport and amenities — only where reliable, geographically specific evidence exists.

  5. 05

    Regulatory and publication readiness

    Applicable statutory records, evidence requirements, permissions and outstanding blockers.

Market gate

A market is not active until it qualifies.

A market-specific gate evaluates required evidence and statutory records. An open blocker prevents activation. Passing the gate does not automatically approve a property, guarantee returns or replace professional legal advice.

Sources

Traceable sources. Explicit limitations.

Market evidence identifies the originating institution, source title, reporting period, retrieval date, unit and verification status. Where an official source cannot be accessed, that limitation is disclosed.
