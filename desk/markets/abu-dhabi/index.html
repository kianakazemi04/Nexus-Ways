Buying rules

Buying here as a foreign buyer

A short summary of official government guidance. Rules can change, so each purchase is confirmed with the developer and the registry at the time.

  • Foreigners may own property in designated investment zones (Law No. 19 of 2005, amended in 2019). There were 50 zones open to all nationalities in mid-2026.
  • Other forms of rights include musataha (50 years, renewable), usufruct (up to 99 years) and long lease (25 years or more).
  • Projects are registered with the Abu Dhabi Real Estate Centre (ADREC).
  • Property investors with a minimum investment of AED 2 million may apply for a five-year UAE residence visa (Golden Visa).

Official source: The Official Portal of the UAE Government (u.ae) · Checked on 29 October 2026.
