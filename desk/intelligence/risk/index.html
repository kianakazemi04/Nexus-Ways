Risk

Documented risks without scores or probabilities.

Evaluation frameworkDecision enginesEvidence and sourcesPricingRiskLiquidityData gaps

Each opportunity carries a documented risk list. A risk records its type, cause, supporting evidence, horizon, controllability, mitigation or diligence action, and residual uncertainty.

What is not shown

There is no risk score and no probability percentage. Severity and likelihood labels require an approved severity method; until it exists they are shown as not assessed. Missing evidence is never read as low risk.

Typical risk types

Legal and title — title type, encumbrances, permits.

Developer and counterparty — legal identity, delivery record.

Construction and delivery — progress, delivery terms.

Regulatory and tax — rules that affect buyers.

Currency — exposure between purchase and home currency.

Seismic and physical — building standards and site conditions.
