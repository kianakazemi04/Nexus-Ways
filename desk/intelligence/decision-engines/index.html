Decision engines

How research, calculation and publication are separated, and the actual status of every method.

Evaluation frameworkDecision enginesEvidence and sourcesPricingRiskLiquidityData gaps

Nexus Desk separates research, calculation and publication. Eight decision engines work on the same evidence records. The status table below is generated from the method registry when the site is built, so it changes only when the implementation changes.

How the engines connect

  1. Evidence and data quality checks every input: source, tier, dates, verification, conflicts and freshness.
  2. Market and location and project, developer and brand organise the evidence by intelligence domain, with Research Closure and Data Completeness recorded separately.
  3. Pricing and comparables and rental and financial scenarios run on private inputs, each with recorded provenance.
  4. Risk and exit documents risks qualitatively, including resale and exit uncertainty.
  5. Comparison and investment fit checks explicit criteria. There is no score and no ranking.
  6. The decision report assembles supported findings, gaps and limitations, citing every record it relies on.

Financial modelling boundaries

ROI and NPV are separate calculations with written definitions. ROI is total, unlevered and undiscounted over a stated horizon; net rental yield is reported separately. NPV discounts end-of-year cash flows at a rate whose basis must be written down, never a default, a mortgage rate or inflation. Every input cites its evidence or is recorded as an explicit assumption; a missing input produces no number. Results are scenarios conditional on their inputs, not forecasts. They are not published and support consultation.

What stays inactive

Ranking, numeric risk scores, fair value, asset-quality and liquidity ratings stay inactive until their method contracts, minimum evidence requirements and tests are complete.
