Data gaps

Missing-data states, and why closure differs from completeness.

Evaluation frameworkEvidence and sourcesPricingRiskLiquidityData gaps

Missing information is recorded explicitly for each field. A field either has a value backed by evidence, or one of four states:

Data not available

No usable data exists for this field.

Accessible, not yet collected

The data exists and can be obtained; research has not reached it yet.

Professional due diligence required

Only a qualified professional (legal, technical, tax) can establish it.

Not applicable

The field does not apply to this record.

Research Closure and Data Completeness

These are independent. Research Closure says whether the research questions for a domain have been answered as far as currently possible; Data Completeness says whether the data itself is complete. Research can be closed while data remains incomplete — for example, when a figure is known not to be published anywhere.

Missing data never becomes zero, a positive or negative score, or a match.
