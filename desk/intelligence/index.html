Workflow

From documents to a traceable recommendation

Six steps, each tied to the methods that actually run. Labels come from the method registry: public results are shown on Desk; internal results support consultation only and are never published; inactive methods are not calculated.

  1. 01

    Evidence

    Every fact carries its source, tier, period, unit, verification status and review date. Conflicts stay visible until explained; gaps keep an explicit state instead of a blank.

    Evidence classification and verification · Active · shown on DeskResearch Closure and Data Completeness · Active · shown on DeskMissing-data states · Active · shown on Desk
  2. 02

    Market and location

    Named official statistics per market, district research and statutory checks feed the market gate. A market opens only when its own gate passes.

    Market publication gate · Not activeStructured domain assessment (market, location, project, developer) · Internal · calculated for research, not publishedCurrency and unit conversion · Internal · calculated for research, not publishedDistrict and area ranking · Not active
  3. 03

    Project and developer

    Identity, developer registration, project registration and the sales office’s written permission are checked before anything is shown.

    Opportunity and media publication gate · Active · shown on DeskAsset Quality assessment · Not active
  4. 04

    Prices and returns

    Comparables, cash flow, ROI, NPV and scenarios are calculated from evidence with stated assumptions, then kept private for consultation.

    Comparable selection and price-per-m² summary (unadjusted) · Internal · calculated for research, not publishedCash-flow model (unlevered, annual) · Internal · calculated for research, not publishedReturn on investment (total, unlevered, undiscounted) · Internal · calculated for research, not publishedNet present value · Internal · calculated for research, not publishedBase, favourable and adverse scenarios · Internal · calculated for research, not publishedFair value · Not activeRental yield and return scenarios · Not active
  5. 05

    Risk and exit

    Documented risks and a qualitative exit review: resale depth, delivery timing and holding constraints, with the evidence behind each point. No risk score, no liquidity estimate.

    Documented risk list (qualitative, no score) · Active · shown on DeskLiquidity and exit review (qualitative) · Internal · calculated for research, not publishedRisk severity and likelihood labels · Not activeLiquidity assessment · Not active
  6. 06

    Fit, comparison and report

    The investor’s objectives and budget are checked criterion by criterion, opportunities are compared side by side, and a private decision report records every number’s source and every missing item.

    Investment Fit (qualitative criteria) · Internal · calculated for research, not publishedBudget eligibility from internal price evidence · Internal · calculated for research, not publishedOpportunity comparison and research queue · Internal · calculated for research, not publishedDecision report (traceable, internal) · Internal · calculated for research, not published
