Intelligence

How we evaluate.

A clear process behind every opportunity we present, and a private analysis prepared for your objectives.

Process

Four steps behind every opportunity

  1. 01

    Verify

    Project identity, developer and registration are checked against official records. What a developer or agent states is labelled as their statement.

  2. 02

    Research the market and location

    Official statistics, registries and market data are compared across sources and kept current for each market and area.

  3. 03

    State the risks

    Every opportunity shows its material risks and what is still being confirmed, in plain language.

  4. 04

    Prepare your private analysis

    Pricing, payment plans, comparable projects and financial scenarios are prepared for your objectives and discussed with you one to one.

Public and private

What you see here, and what we prepare for you

On Desk
  • Verified project and developer facts
  • Location and delivery status
  • Material risks and open points
  • The sources behind the facts
In your consultation
  • Current pricing and payment plans
  • Comparable projects in the area
  • Financial scenarios with stated assumptions
  • Fit with your objectives, budget and timeframe

Pricing changes often and depends on the unit and the terms, so it is shared directly, with its date and source.

Labels

How facts are labelled

VerifiedConfirmed against an official or primary record.
CorroboratedConsistent across independent sources.
Source-reportedReported by one credible source; not yet independently confirmed.
Developer claimStated by the developer or its agents.
Sources

Sources we use

Official publishers and registries in each market. Their figures support our analysis and are discussed in consultation; Desk does not republish them.

Istanbul
  • Turkish Statistical Institute (TÜİK)
  • Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye (TCMB)
  • General Directorate of Land Registry and Cadastre (TKGM)
  • District municipalities (building permits)
Dubai
  • Dubai Land Department (DLD) and RERA
  • Dubai Pulse (DLD transaction data)
  • Dubai Statistics Center
  • Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE)
Abu Dhabi
  • Abu Dhabi Real Estate Centre (ADREC)
  • Department of Municipalities and Transport (DMT)
  • Statistics Centre – Abu Dhabi (SCAD)
  • Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE)

Market reports from established research firms and the financial press, used to cross-check — never as the only source.
